# Mental Maths – Grade 2
Interactive practice with automatic marking and step-by-step explanations for Grade 2 and Grade 3 (choose the grade at the top of the menu).
Number questions and short-answer questions (number names, days, months, shapes) are taken from the "Cambridge Mental Maths Grade 2" solutions workbook (Terms 1–4).

## Run
Open `index.html` in a browser (keep `questions.js` in the same folder).

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Create a repository and upload all files, `parse_text.py`, `extra_text.py`, `textquestions.js`. Term 5 in the menu is "Extra: words & facts" (questions written for this app in the same style).
2. Settings → Pages → Branch: `main` / root → Save.
3. Your site will be at `https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/`.

## Add more questions
Add objects to `questions.js`: `{t:Term,w:Week,a:Activity,n:"Title",q:"12 + 8",r:20}`.

Short-answer questions go in `textquestions.js`: `{t:1,w:1,a:1,n:"Title",q:"Question?",r:["accepted answer","other accepted answer"],e:"Explanation"}` (answers in lower case).

## Grade 3
`grade3.js` holds the Grade 3 questions, grouped into the 17 units of the Cambridge Primary Mathematics Workbook 3. They are generated by `gen_grade3.py` (run `python3 gen_grade3.py` to rebuild the file). Upload `grade3.js` next to `index.html`.

## Grade 2 – Workbook (by unit)
`grade2wb.js` follows the 15 units of Cambridge Primary Mathematics Workbook 2 (sums printed in the book plus extra practice). Rebuild it with `gen_grade2_workbook.py`.

## Grade 4
`grade4.js` follows the 18 units of the Cambridge Primary Mathematics Workbook 4 (sums printed in the book plus extra practice). Rebuild it with `gen_grade4.py`.
